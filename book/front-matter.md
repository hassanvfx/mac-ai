# About the Author

Hassan Uriostegui is a Mexican-American technologist and author whose work
spans mobile video, visual effects, AI-human interaction, and AI research. He
builds practical systems at the meeting point of creative technology and
machine intelligence, and his work with Waken AI Labs explores responsible
human-centered AI. This book turns that systems perspective into a reproducible
learning path for Apple Silicon developers.

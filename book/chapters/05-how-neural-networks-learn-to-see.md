# How Neural Networks Learn to See

## Intuition

Convolutions use local structure and repeated filters to turn pixels into useful
features.

## Problem

Train and inspect a small image classifier rather than treating a CNN as a black box.

## Minimal implementation

The companion experiment will train a CNN on a reproducible image dataset.

## Experiment

Compare accuracy, confusion cases, and the effect of augmentation.

## What broke

Capture data-loading and overfitting failures.

## Alternatives and takeaway

Inductive bias can make a model simpler and more data-efficient.

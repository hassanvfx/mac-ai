# Building an AI System You Can Actually Trust

## Intuition

Reliability comes from evaluation, observability, constraints, and recovery—not
from a single impressive demo.

## Problem

Integrate local retrieval, optional models, planning, critique, human approval,
and evaluation into a Book Intelligence Assistant that can be inspected and
improved.

## Minimal implementation

The companion capstone retains a frozen fixture corpus, versioned evaluation
questions, traces, and explicit acceptance checks.

## Experiment

Measure answer quality, latency, failure rate, and operator interventions.

## What broke

Record hallucinations, tool errors, unavailable models, and recovery behavior.

## Alternatives and takeaway

Trustworthy AI systems treat probabilistic output as one component in a broader
engineering discipline.

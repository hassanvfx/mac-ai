# Generative AI Lab — Source Notes

## Scope

Chapter 15 uses two external, author-maintained repositories as linked case
studies. They are not vendored into this companion repository and are not
benchmark fixtures.

## Claims carried into the manuscript

- ClineFlow documents an optional, Git-native project-memory workflow and an
  OKF knowledge bundle [@clineflow2026].
- Google Cloud describes OKF as an open, vendor-neutral representation based on
  Markdown, YAML front matter, links, and version-controlled files
  [@google2026okf].
- Lyrics Refiner documents a staged local arrangement workflow and a
  deterministic source-word preservation check [@lyricsrefiner2026;
  @lyricsrefinerarticle2026].
- Newsmusic documents dry-run operation, local creator-owned credentials,
  disabled third-party-footage download, and private review-gated delivery
  [@newsmusic2026; @newsmusicarticle2026].

## Boundaries

Do not infer performance, cost, legal clearance, artistic quality, audience
growth, or production scale from these sources. Treat credentials, live model
calls, media rights, factual assertions, and public publishing as explicit human
responsibilities.
